## Mockup
- Draw a simplified screen: a frame, a header bar, cards, rows and buttons as rounded
  rectangles (`rx` 4 to 8).
- The frame uses `fill-bg` with `stroke-grid`; panels use `fill-tint`; one button uses
  `fill-accent` with text `fill-bg`.
- Use short realistic labels. Use grey bars (`fill-grid`) for text lines that need no words.
- Keep three levels of detail at most: frame, panels, items.
